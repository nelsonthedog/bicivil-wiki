Getting Started

1. Connect

  1. Open Minecraft Java Edition (1.21.x).
  2. Multiplayer → Add Server → enter play.bcivil.example.

2. Spawn

Read the signs at spawn, then use /spawn any time to return.

3. Settle and protect

Find a spot, build a shelter, and claim your land.

4. Join the community

Hop in Discord, meet your neighbours, and check the community page.
