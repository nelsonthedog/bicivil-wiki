====== Server Rules ======

Breaking these rules can result in a warning, mute, kick, or ban.

  - **Be respectful.** No harassment, hate speech, or toxicity.
  - **No griefing or stealing.** Do not damage or take from builds that aren't yours, unless claim rules say otherwise.
  - **No cheating.** X-ray, hacked clients, fly/speed hacks, and duping are bannable.
  - **No exploiting bugs.** Report them to staff instead.
  - **No spam or advertising** in chat.
  - **No lag machines.** Keep redstone and mob farms reasonable.
  - **Follow staff instructions.**

===== Punishments =====

^ Offence ^ Typical result ^
| Minor chat offence | Warning / mute |
| Griefing | Rollback + temp ban |
| Cheating | Permanent ban |
