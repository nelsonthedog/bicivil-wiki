====== Editing Help ======

Full reference: [[https://www.dokuwiki.org/wiki:syntax|DokuWiki syntax]]

^ You type ^ You get ^
| ''**bold**'' | **bold** |
| ''//italic//'' | //italic// |
| ''[[server:rules|Rules]]'' | link to a page |
| ''  * item'' | bullet list |
| ''  - item'' | numbered list |
| ''====== Title ======'' | page heading |

Pages are plain ''.txt'' files in ''content/pages/''. Namespaces are folders: ''[[guides:claims]]'' is ''guides/claims.txt''.
