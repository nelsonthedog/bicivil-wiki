====== Getting Started ======

===== 1. Connect =====

  - Open Minecraft Java Edition (1.21.x).
  - Multiplayer -> Add Server -> enter ''play.bcivil.example''.

===== 2. Spawn =====

Read the signs at spawn, then use ''/spawn'' any time to return.

===== 3. Settle and protect =====

Find a spot, build a shelter, and [[guides:claims|claim your land]].

===== 4. Join the community =====

Hop in Discord, meet your neighbours, and check the [[community:start|community page]].
